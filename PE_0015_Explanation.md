#Project Euler - Lattice Paths

For us to move from the top left corner to bottom right corner , we can only use 40 moves( 20 down and 20 right).
So the different ways in which we can go is just in the difference of the moves.
So we need to do a total of 40 moves out of which 2 groups of 20 are alike.
So , no of ways = (40!)/(20!)(20!).
