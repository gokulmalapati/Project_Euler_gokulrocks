#Project Euler - Highly Divisible Triangular Number

The triangular number is just consequetive sum of the numbers , so i simplified it using n(n+1)/2
I then used a while loop and is_Prime function to find the power of the respective prime less than the number.
And the total no. of actors of number is taken as , eg., x = (2^a)(3^b)... , no. of factors would be (a+1)(b+1)...
We caluclated no. of factors using the variable called product
The entire operation is in a while loop which terminates when Product i.e, no. of factors crosses 500.
